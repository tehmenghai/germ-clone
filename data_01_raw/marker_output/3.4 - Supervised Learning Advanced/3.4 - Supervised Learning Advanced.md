## What is Regularization?

## Regularization is a technique used to

## prevent overﬁtting in machine learning

## models by penalizing complex models.

## It involves adding a regularization term

## (penalty)  to the cost function to

## discourage the model from learning

## overly complex patterns that just ﬁt

## the training data.

## Types of Regularization

## L1 (Lasso)

## Adds the sum of the absolute values

## of the coeﬃcients to the loss

## function.

## It can lead to sparse models where

## some feature coeﬃcients are zeroed

## out, eﬀectively performing feature

## selection.

## L2 (Ridge)

## L2 regularization adds the sum of the

## squares of the coeﬃcients to the loss

## function.

## It doesn't zero out coeﬃcients but

## ensures they are small, leading to a

## less complex model.

## Grid Search

## Grid search is a brute force method to estimate the best hyperparameters.

## A grid of hyperparameter combinations is created, and the model is

## evaluated for each combination using cross-validation.

## 1.

## Deﬁne the hyperparameter grid.

## 2.

## Use cross-validation to evaluate

## each combination of

## hyperparameters.

## 3.

## Select the combination that yields

## the best performance.


<!-- page 21 -->
Copyright © 2024 Nanyang Technological University. All rights reserved.

## Random Search

## Random search is an alternative to

## grid search. Instead of trying out every

## possible combination, it samples a

## random subset of parameter

## combinations.

## •

## Can be faster than grid search

## when dealing with a large

## hyperparameter space.

## •

## Can sometimes ﬁnd a better

## combination of hyperparameters

## by exploring a wider range of

## values.

## 1.

## Deﬁne a search space as a bounded

## domain of hyperparameter values.

## 2.

## Randomly sample combinations of

## hyperparameters from this domain.

## 3.

## Perform cross-validation for each

## combination.

## 4.

## Select the combination that yields

## the best validation score.

## Model Training Workﬂow

## Step 1
After preprocessing of
the data, train-test split
is performed.
The test set is kept for
the ﬁnal evaluation of
the tuned model.

## Step 2
A combination of
hyperparameters'
prospective values is
determined.
Then grid/random search
is performed to
determine the best
possible combination
candidate by using
cross-validation to
evaluate each candidate.

## Step 3
The whole training set is
used to train the model
with the best candidate
combination of
hyperparameters found
from the previous step.
This model is called the
hyperparameter-tuned
model.

## Step 4
Evaluate the tuned
model on the test set.

## What is LightGBM

## •

## LightGBM ( Light G radient B oosting M achine) is a gradient

## boosting framework developed by Microsoft that uses

## tree-based learning algorithms and is designed for distributed

## and eﬃcient training, particularly on large datasets.

## •

## LightGBM is known for its speed and performance, as well as

## its ability to handle large-sized data and support GPU learning.

## •

## Currently one of the best Supervised Learning algorithms in

## terms of performance and speed for tabular (structural or

## dataframe-like) datasets.
