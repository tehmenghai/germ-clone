## Sum of Squares in Linear Regression

## Sum of Squares

## Total (SST)

## Measures total variation

## in dependent variable

## SST = Σ(yᵢ - ȳ)²

## Sum of Squares of

## Residuals (SSR)

## Measures unexplained

## variation

## SSR = Σ(yᵢ - ŷᵢ)²
SST is your baseline for
understanding how much “noise”
exists in the data.
SSR tells you how much variation your

## model failed to explain. A smaller SSR
means your model ﬁts the data better.

## R-Squared (R²) in Linear Regression

## SST = Total variation

## SSR = Residual variation

## Value range: 0 to 1

## Interpretation :

## Higher R² indicates better ﬁt

## Limitation:

## High R² doesn't guarantee

## appropriate model

## (overﬁtting risk)

## R² = 1 - (SSR/SST)
